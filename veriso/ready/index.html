Veriso Ready — Entry point · Mobilisation

Is the contractor actually ready to mobilise?

Ready checks that contractors are genuinely prepared before they start — with proof, not a checklist copied from a template.

Qualify Ready Control Process Comply Environment

What it does

Gates that mean what the tender said.

01

Gates inherit the tender

Everything the contractor promised at tender automatically becomes the list of things they must prove before starting — same contractor, same record.

02

Each gate clears on evidence

People, equipment and supervision are checked as they arrive on site — where, when and by whom. A gate is passed or it is not; there is no "nearly".

03

Day one starts proven

The proof gathered at the gates becomes the starting point of the live control register — so nothing proven once has to be proven again.

What you hold at the end

The output

Output
A mobilisation gate record — every gate, its requirement, its evidence, its verdict, and the name of the person who cleared it.
What it removes
Mobilisation by momentum: contractors starting because the programme needed them to start, with readiness asserted rather than shown.
Who reads it
Programme leadership sees what is blocking. The contractor sees exactly what is required and why. The board sees that no one started unproven.
Handoff
Ready → Control. Gate evidence becomes the opening state of the live critical-control register on day one.

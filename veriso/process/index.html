What it does

The safety case, kept honest.

01

Barriers, itemised from your studies

Your existing process safety studies become a live list of barriers — each with what it must do, how to test it, and the document it came from.

02

Field verification against the standard

Barriers are checked where they stand, by named people, with a place and a time. If the study underneath is out of date, the score says so.

03

One language across the interface

Construction and operations on the same site are checked the same way — so nothing gets lost between two different systems at the handover.

What you hold at the end

Output
A barrier register for the operating asset — current status, degraded bases flagged, every verdict anchored to the study clause it tests.

Technical authorities see barrier status against performance standards. Duty holders see what they are accountable for today, not at last audit.

Should this contractor be working for you at all?

Qualify replaces scored questionnaires with proof. We read the whole tender, trace every claim to its source, and rank contractors on what can be verified — not on what was written well.

Qualify Ready Control Process Comply Environment

What it does

Selection on evidence, not eloquence.

01

Submissions are read whole

The system reads every tender document in full, with senior people checking its judgment. Every claim — safety record, competence, equipment, finances — is pulled out and linked to the page it came from.

02

Claims become testable

What a contractor promises becomes a list of testable requirements. The controls that prevent fatalities are named individually, so nothing life-threatening hides inside an average score.

03

Promises survive award

At contract award, nothing is filed away and forgotten. The promises carry straight through to mobilisation — same contractor, same record, no restart.

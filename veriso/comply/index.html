Is every worker in the chain employed and treated lawfully?

Comply maps every worker on your site back to the company that actually employs them, so responsibility does not stop at the main contractor's signature.

Qualify Ready Control Process Comply Environment

What it does

The chain, mapped to the last employer.

01

Every employer identified

The chain is mapped layer by layer, from main contractor to subcontractors, labour suppliers and brokers, until every worker is tied to the company that actually employs them.

02

Standing verified, not declared

Licensing, wages, accommodation and welfare are checked at each employer in the chain. Worker feedback is pooled before anyone sees it, so no individual can be identified.

03

Accountability follows the chain

Responsibility attaches to the company that employs the worker, not just the one that signed the head contract. The record does not stop at the signature.

What you hold at the end

The output

Output
A labour-chain map with verified standing at each employer, plus an explicit record of where the chain could not be reached, reported as uncovered rather than averaged away.
What it removes
The labour-supply blind spot: workers on your site employed by entities you have never heard of, under conditions you have never seen.
Who reads it
Programme leadership sees chain integrity. Lenders and DFIs see E&S standing with evidence behind it. No output ever isolates an individual.
Handoff
Comply → all four. The chain map informs contractor standing, gate ownership and control accountability across every other product.

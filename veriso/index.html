How it works

Documents record intent. The register records reality.

Step 1

Your documents become controls

Your existing studies and plans become a list of clear, checkable actions — each one linked back to the exact document it came from.

Step 2

Controls go to named owners

Each action goes to a named person who owns it. The system suggests; the person decides — and the decision is recorded.

Step 3

Field evidence comes back

Checks come back from the field with a place, a time and a name. What you see is not what people plan to do — it is what is actually protecting your people today.

The architecture

Whichever door you enter, the evidence lands in the same register.

One standard of proof. A check is a check — whether it is a safety barrier, a lifting control or a worker accommodation visit.

One way of scoring. Every product uses the same scale, so you can compare across projects without guesswork.

Set up for you, not rebuilt for you. Your branding, your requirements, your sites — without custom software to maintain.

Your data stays yours. The records, the evidence and the setup belong to you — you can take them with you at any time.

The confidence model

Every output carries how much you can trust it.

Every finding carries one of four simple bands, so you always know how much weight to put on it. The system applies them automatically — nobody can upgrade a number because a deadline is near.

Band 01

Not assured

Not checked yet — and shown as not checked, never hidden inside an average.

Band 02

Limited confidence

Based on documents alone, however complete they look. Paperwork can only ever reach this band — proof from the field goes further.

Band 03 · No method ceiling

Reasonable confidence

Checked in person, on site — with a place, a time and a name attached, on documents that are current.

Band 04

Basis degraded

Checked in person, but the document underneath is out of date — so the score is capped until it is refreshed.

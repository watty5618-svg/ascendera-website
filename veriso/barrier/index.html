How it works

From study to live system in days.

The system reads the studies you have already paid for. Nobody re-types anything — your analysis becomes the system.

01

Your studies, read in full

The system reads your existing process safety studies end to end and pulls out every hazard and every safeguard — each one linked back to the exact page it came from.

02

A clear picture of every risk

Each risk is laid out simply: what could cause it, what could happen, and what stands in between. Your specialists review and refine — a running start, not a blank page.

03

The picture goes live

Every barrier gets a named owner and a current status, checked in the field — the same standard of proof as every other Veriso product.

04

Change keeps it true

Every change to the plant is checked against the barriers it affects, so the picture always matches the plant as it is — never as it was.

System understanding

One picture of risk, at every depth.

The board sees how well the plant is protected. Your engineers see the detail underneath. The control room sees what must work today. Everyone reads the same picture — at the depth their job needs.

That is what turns process safety from a study you commission every few years into something you manage every day.

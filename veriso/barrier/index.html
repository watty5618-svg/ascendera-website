How it works

From study to live system in days.

The system reads the studies you have already paid for. Nobody re-types anything: your analysis becomes the system.

01

Your studies, read in full

The system reads your existing process safety studies end to end and pulls out every hazard and every safeguard, each one linked back to the exact page it came from.

02

A clear picture of every risk

Each risk is laid out simply: what could cause it, what could happen, and what stands in between. Your specialists review and refine, so it is a running start, not a blank page.

03

The picture goes live

Every barrier gets a named owner and a current status, checked in the field to the same standard of proof as every other Veriso product.

04

Change keeps it true

Every change to the plant is checked against the barriers it affects, so the picture always matches the plant as it is, never as it was.

System understanding

One picture of risk, at every depth.

The board sees how well the plant is protected. Your engineers see the detail underneath. The control room sees what must work today. Everyone reads the same picture, at the depth their job needs.

That is what turns process safety from a study you commission every few years into something you manage every day.

What you hold at the end

The output

Output
A live list of every barrier on your plant: who owns it, how well it is working, and when it was last checked, all linked back to the study it came from.
Speed
Up and running in days rather than weeks, because the system reads your studies instead of a team re-typing them.
Change management
Every plant change is checked against the barriers it touches. Nothing quietly goes out of date, because you are told the moment it does.
One shared picture
Hazards, barriers and people connected in one view: the same picture for the board, your engineers and the control room.
Who it serves
Operating plants in energy, petrochemicals, utilities and mining, anywhere safety depends on barriers working.
One record
Barrier status lands in the same record as every other Veriso product, scored the same way, so there is one language across your sites and plants.

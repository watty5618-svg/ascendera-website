Veriso Platform in practice

From contractor selection and mobilisation to field controls, welfare and management-system improvement. See how selected modules support different decisions.

A developer needs to select a contractor. A project needs to mobilise. An operator needs evidence that critical controls are in place. Veriso Platform can be scoped around these different decisions, using selected modules or the wider platform.

For pre-qualification, examine the contractor's capability against the requirements of the work. Connect supporting evidence to the criteria and make unresolved gaps visible. The award team can distinguish demonstrated capability from a submission that simply reads well.

For mobilisation and commissioning, scope the checks to the activity about to begin. Examine equipment, competence, permits and required safeguards. Identify what is ready, what is missing and who must act before the authorised team makes its start decision.

During construction, focus on the controls behind work at height, moving plant and pedestrians, confined spaces and excavation. Field observations test whether the specified controls are present and working. A completed procedure and a control observed in practice remain different evidence.

You can focus on environmental controls, social commitments or worker welfare. Match the scope to the obligations and activities involved. Follow evidence from the requirement to the observation, the gap and its owner, without needing every other module at the outset.

For a wider improvement programme, compare requirements, the management system and what happens on site. Produce proposed changes in the organisation's own format. Owners review them, accepted changes inform training, and later field checks test whether the improvement reached the work.

A site team sees the actions it owns. Functional leaders see recurring gaps across projects. Decision-makers see significant conditions and unresolved responsibilities. Selected modules can share the same evidence method, while each view keeps its scope, date and coverage clear.

Start with pre-qualification, mobilisation, critical risk, commissioning, environment or social assurance. Extend when the work calls for it. The same approach can support construction, manufacturing and operations: understand what is required, examine the evidence and make the next action clear.

The doctrine

Six rules that cannot be waived by instruction.

These are enforced in the engine rather than left to reviewer diligence — and they are the reason a Veriso output can be relied on in a boardroom, by a lender, and after an incident. They hold under pressure, which is precisely when they matter.

Rule 01
Nothing counts on paper alone. No safety control is reported as working just because a document says so. It counts when a competent person has checked it in the field, on documents that are current.
Rule 02
Missing is shown as missing. Where proof does not exist, the report says so. We never fill gaps with reputation, seniority or assumption.
Rule 03
Scores reflect the strength of the proof. Findings from documents alone can only reach limited confidence. Only checks done in the field — with a place, a time and a name — reach reasonable confidence.
Rule 04
Everything links back to its source. Every finding traces to the exact document and page it came from, so your own people can check anything we say without asking us.
Rule 05
The right reader gets the right page. The board, your engineers and the workface each see material built for them — automatically.
Rule 06
The system suggests, people decide. Every decision that matters belongs to a named, accountable person and is recorded against their name. The product supports those decisions; it never makes them.

The confidence bands

How much you can trust every output.

The band names come from the same vocabulary auditors use for assurance engagements — so your board, your lenders and your auditors already speak the language. The system applies the bands automatically; nobody can adjust them by hand.

Band 01

Not assured

Not checked yet — and shown as not checked, never hidden inside an average or a score.

Band 02

Limited confidence

Based on documents alone, however complete they look. Paperwork can only ever reach this band — proof from the field goes further.

Band 03 · No method ceiling

Reasonable confidence

Checked in person, on site — with a place, a time and a name attached, on documents that are current.

Band 04

Basis degraded

Checked in person, but the document underneath is out of date — so the score is capped until it is refreshed.

Scope, stated plainly

Scope, in one paragraph.

Veriso gathers evidence and reports it. It does not issue certificates, fulfil a legal duty on your behalf, or give permission for work to start or continue. Findings from documents alone are marked limited. Where a site or contractor could not be reached, the report says so rather than hiding it in an average. A competent person reviews and signs, and the accountable person decides.

The honest test

Six rules. Zero exceptions.

The same rules apply to every client, every product and every deadline. That consistency is what makes a Veriso figure worth putting in front of a board.

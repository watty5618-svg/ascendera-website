VERISO | Assurance Platform
Running time: 07:15.320

00:00.000 | Across industries, around the work
Across construction, giga projects, manufacturing, operations and other high-hazard industries, the same questions matter. What are we required to do? Does our management system provide for it? And is it happening where the work takes place? Veriso Platform connects those questions. It turns information, requirements and field evidence into a continuous cycle of learning, action and verification, with the scope matched to the hazards and work involved.

00:30.560 | The whole architecture, in one view
The architecture brings company information in, assesses it against the requirements that apply, and maps the gaps. It then produces the changes, training and improvement actions in the organisation's own way of working. Field observations test the result. Leadership uses that evidence to set a North Star, strategy and roadmap, and the next cycle begins with a clearer picture.

00:54.000 | Bring in the system you already use
Start with the material already used to run the organisation: policies, procedures, forms, risk assessments, training records, permits, maintenance information and incident records. Reviewed scans and photographs can bring older material into the picture too. Veriso builds an inventory and system profile, preserving sources, versions, terminology, document structure and the organisation's own working language.

01:23.840 | Understand the requirements that apply
The comparison is built around the organisation's context. Country-specific legal and regulatory requirements sit alongside applicable codes, standards, client conditions, lender requirements and company commitments. The organisation's own methods, responsibilities and approved controls remain part of the picture. Applicability is established for the jurisdiction, activity and scope, so the assessment is grounded in what actually applies.

01:53.280 | Produce the gap map automatically
Veriso analyses the material and produces a linked gap map. It shows where a requirement is addressed, partly addressed, missing or contradicted, and where a control exists only on paper. Every gap points back to the requirement and the relevant source. Teams can see what needs to change in their own system, with the basis for each proposed finding available to review.

02:17.520 | Separate system provision from ground truth
The second comparison is with ground truth. A procedure may be complete while the control is absent in practice. A team may also be doing useful work that its system fails to capture. Veriso keeps required controls, documented provision and observed practice distinct. Their differences show whether the problem is missing design, unclear direction, poor implementation or insufficient evidence.

02:43.040 | Turn the North Star into a practical route
Leadership sets the North Star: the outcomes the organisation is trying to achieve. Veriso connects that direction to the evidence. It orders gaps and improvement priorities by consequence, requirements, dependencies and what remains unverified. Strategy becomes a roadmap. The roadmap becomes practical plans, named owners, actions and review dates, with local routes that support the same organisational direction.

03:12.160 | Create the response in your own format
The platform produces the response in the organisation's own structure and format. It drafts tracked changes to existing procedures, creates missing documents and recommends improvements to the information captured in existing systems. Terminology, roles, templates and house style follow the company profile. Each proposed change is connected to the gap it addresses and the requirement behind it.

03:36.720 | The owner adopts the change
The responsible owner reviews what has been produced. They can accept it, edit it or reject it with a recorded reason. The decision stays connected to the original gap. Accepted changes enter the organisation's controlled adoption process. Rejected or unresolved items stay visible, so a convincing piece of writing cannot silently close a real management-system weakness.

04:00.040 | Create training from the accepted change
From accepted changes, Veriso prepares role-specific training programmes: briefings, toolbox talks, learning material and checks of understanding. The organisation reviews and adopts that material through the same controlled process, then uses its existing learning system to deliver it. People receive an explanation of what has changed and what they need to do. Completion is recorded, while competence and performance still require appropriate evidence.

04:27.800 | Verify high-risk construction work
The next step happens where the work takes place. On a construction project, that includes work at height, the interface between mobile plant and pedestrians, confined space work and excavation. A competent observer uses the prescribed test for the control and records what was found, with its evidence, context and the observer's role. The same evidence method applies across sectors, using the controls relevant to each activity. A revised procedure, delivered training and a control observed in practice remain distinct.

05:00.760 | Evidence determines the assurance view
AI assists with reading, drafting and explanation. Assurance results come from the governing rules applied to sufficient admissible observations. The result can be traced to its evidence, the rule version and the time it was assessed. Sealed records make later alteration evident. They preserve the basis of the conclusion while leaving the quality and sufficiency of the evidence open to examination.

05:25.560 | One record, useful at every level
The same record supports different decisions. A site team sees what needs action. Functional leaders see patterns and coverage. The board sees significant gaps, carried risk and decisions requiring ownership. Authorised stakeholders can examine the relevant evidence. Counts show their totals, and serious deficiencies remain visible. The result is a shared basis for decisions without hiding uncertainty inside one overall score.

05:52.960 | Six areas of assurance
Six modules apply the approach to different questions. Qualify examines contractor capability before award. Ready checks the conditions for the first task. Control examines critical controls during work. Process addresses assurance of major-accident barriers. Social and Welfare covers workers' conditions across their employment journey. Environment addresses the controls behind environmental commitments. Organisations select the scope they need while keeping a common evidence method.

06:21.880 | Learn, reset and improve
The cycle continues as requirements change, work changes and new evidence arrives. Gaps reopen when their basis no longer holds. The organisation learns from recurring findings, adjusts its strategy and updates the roadmap. Veriso connects reading, measurement, ground truth, direction, improvement, checking and reporting into one repeatable cycle. Each pass improves the picture of the system and the reality it is meant to support.

06:51.960 | A system that learns from the work
Veriso Platform connects what is required, what your system provides and what happens in practice. It helps you find the gaps, build the response in your own format, prepare your people and verify the result. With a North Star and a practical route to improvement, the organisation gains more than another set of documents. It gains a system that can learn from the work.

The idea

A classroom tells you who attended. A campus tells you who can do the work.

Kalibra closes the last gap in the assurance chain. Advisory maps the route. Veriso verifies the controls. Kalibra proves the people — before they step onto your site.

01

Real conditions, built for learning

Height, confined spaces, live plant and fatigue. The scenarios mirror what your people will actually face — so the skills they prove here transfer directly to your site.

02

Named assessors, observable evidence

Every assessment is done by a named, accountable assessor and recorded as evidence — proof that stays useful long after the day itself.

03

Proof that lands in the register

Proof of competence goes straight into the same trusted record as everything else. When a worker arrives at your gate, the evidence of what they can do arrives with them.
